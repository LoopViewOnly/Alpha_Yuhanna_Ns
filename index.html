CAREER MILESTONES

Key defining moments from his debut as a teenager to lifting football's ultimate prize in Qatar.

2004 - 2005

FC Barcelona First Team Debut

Debut at age 17 under Frank Rijkaard, assisted by Ronaldinho for his first official senior goal against Albacete.

2009 - 2012

The 91-Goal World Record & First 4 Ballon d'Ors

Broke Gerd Müller’s 40-year calendar record by scoring 91 goals in 2012. Won four consecutive Ballon d'Or awards.

2015

The MSN Treble Victory

Formed the deadliest trio in football history alongside Luis Suárez and Neymar Jr, lifting UEFA Champions League & Treble.

2021 - 2022

Copa América & FIFA World Cup Glory

Led Argentina to international triumph at Maracanã in 2021, culminating in winning the 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar with 7 goals and the Golden Ball.
